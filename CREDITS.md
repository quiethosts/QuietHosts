# Credits

QuietHosts' block lists, tiers and notes are written by the QuietHosts contributors and
released under the MIT License. Each address was reviewed one by one and checked against the
public lists below, which helped us find candidates and spot breakage. We did not copy
these lists, their comments or their order, and QuietHosts neither includes nor downloads
them. Thank you to their maintainers.

Checked against:

- HaGeZi's DNS Blocklists (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/hagezi/dns-blocklists
- OISD (no license stated on its site; its GitHub repository is GPL-3.0): https://oisd.nl
- AdGuard DNS filter (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/AdguardTeam/AdGuardSDNSFilter
- Peter Lowe's ad and tracking server list: https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/
- EasyList and EasyPrivacy by The EasyList authors (GPL-3.0-or-later or CC BY-SA 3.0-or-later): https://easylist.to/

Used to find breakage: anudeepND/whitelist (MIT), HaGeZi's referral allowlist (GPL-3.0),
and the exception rules of the AdGuard DNS filter, EasyList and EasyPrivacy.
Also consulted, nothing copied: 1Hosts Lite (MPL-2.0), uBlock Origin filters (GPL-3.0),
DuckDuckGo Tracker Radar (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), Infoblox Threat Intelligence (CC BY 4.0),
urlscan.io, superadblocktest.com and adblock.turtlecute.org.
