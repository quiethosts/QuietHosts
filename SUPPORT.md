## Ask or report on GitHub

| You want to | Use |
|---|---|
| Report a site or app that QuietHosts breaks | First try **Fix a site or app…** in QuietHosts, then the [broken-site form](https://github.com/quiethosts/QuietHosts/issues/new?template=site-broke.yml). |
| Report something in QuietHosts that doesn't work | The [problem form](https://github.com/quiethosts/QuietHosts/issues/new?template=bug.yml). |
| Suggest, move or remove an address | A pull request, as [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) describes. |
| Report a security problem | A private report, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes. Never a public issue. |

Good reports help us fix things fast. Please include:

- your QuietHosts version (**About** shows it) and your Windows version (press Windows+R, type `winver` and press Enter);
- what you did, step by step, and what you saw;
- if QuietHosts showed an error, the text from its **Copy details** link. Read it before you post: it lists versions, your Windows build and what went wrong, and your user folder shows as %USERPROFILE%.

Reports are public, and you need a GitHub account to post one. Don't include your name, email address or other personal information.

## What to expect

QuietHosts is a free project. There is no paid support and no support by email, chat or phone: issues on GitHub are the place to ask. We try to answer every report, but it can take a while.

QuietHosts will never ask you to pay, to install other software, or to give anyone remote access to your PC. Anyone who does isn't us.

Everyone who takes part follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
